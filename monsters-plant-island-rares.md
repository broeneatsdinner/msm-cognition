### Single-element Rare breeding rule

For Rare Potbelly, Rare Noggin, Rare Toe Jammer, and Rare Mammott, breed **two different triple-element monsters that both contain the target element**. For example, both parents for Rare Potbelly must contain Plant. A “triple” means a triple-element monster.

## Purchasing and activation notes

- **15 Rare Naturals:** Can be bred during qualifying promotions or purchased when offered in the StarShop or Market.
- **Rare Ghazt and Rare G'joob:** Can be bred during eligible promotions or purchased when offered.
- **Rare Punkleton:** Associated with Spooktacle; breeding and purchasing are limited to eligible event windows.
- **Rare Wubbox:** Must be purchased from the StarShop and activated by boxing the required Rare monsters into it. It cannot be bred.
- **Separate availability:** StarShop offers and breeding promotions operate independently. Check the current in-game announcements and offers before attempting a Rare breeding combination.
