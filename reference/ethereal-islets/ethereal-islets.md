# Ethereal Islets

Last researched: 2026-10-01. Sources: Help Center, Wiki.

## What they are

- Four islets exist so far: Plasma, Mech, Shadow, Crystal. The wiki says five
  are eventually planned, one per Ethereal Element.
- They sit on the Mirror map, surrounding the Mirror Islands, each shaped like
  a hand of the Colossal Zarroe.
- They are the only Mirror Timeline islands that are not variants of a
  main-timeline island.
- Each islet holds all Single, Double and Triple Ethereals of its element, that
  element's Quad boss, and one Primordial. That is twelve Ethereals plus the
  Primordial.
- Structures per islet: one Dish-Harmonizer and two Egg Cups.
- Structures, decorations and paths cannot be placed. The Market sells only
  monsters and costumes there. Obstacles can be removed.

## Unlocking (Help Center)

Either:

1. Teleport the matching Level 14 Quad-Element Monster from Ethereal Workshop
   (Whaill for Plasma, for example), or
2. Spend Shards in the Mirror Map / Islands menu in the Market, once Ethereal
   Workshop is unlocked.

## Teleporting in and out

- **In:** only the islet's own common Quad-Element Ethereal, from Ethereal
  Workshop, at Level 14. Other ethereals cannot be teleported in. Rare and Epic
  monsters cannot be teleported to the islets, so a Rarethereal reaches an islet
  only through the Dish-Harmonizer.
- **To Ethereal Workshop or Ethereal Island:** not possible (Help Center; wiki
  agrees).
- **To the Colossingum:** possible (wiki, Teleporting page). Monsters at Level 5
  or higher, limited to the common Natural Monsters, Single and Double
  Ethereals, and Core Seasonals. Rare and Epic cannot go. It is one-way.
- Teleporting resets level to 1 and the monster returns as an egg in the
  destination nursery or egg cup.
- Monsters never travel between main and Mirror timelines (Help Center).

Correction log: the Help Center article titled "Can I teleport Monsters on
Ethereal Islets to Ethereal Workshop or Ethereal Island?" says monsters "cannot
be teleported to other Islands". Read in context it covers those two islands
only. The Colossingum exception comes from the wiki.

## Monsters and currency

- Monsters on islets are obtained only through Dish-Harmonization. See
  `dish-harmonizer.md`.
- The Dish-Harmonizer has an endless supply of the islet's own Meeb. The other
  four Meebs come from harmonizing monsters.
- Higher-element monsters earn more Shards. A Quad Rarethereal (Rare Whaill on
  Plasma) is described as having excellent Shard production for the islet.
  Exact earning rates have not been captured yet.
- Rarethereals can only be harmonized during special promotions, usually on
  weekends.
- A Primordial of the same rarity on an islet removes the need to place liked
  items near a monster, as a Unity Tree does elsewhere.

## Song

The Dish-Harmonizer adds a bass line to the islet song once the Single, a
Double, a Triple and the Quad are collected.

## Player state

Plasma, Crystal and Shadow Islets are unlocked and Mech Islet is locked as of
the 2026-08-23 snapshot. See `../islands/player-map-2026-08-23.md`.
