# Dish-Harmonizer

Last researched: 2026-10-01. Sources: Help Center, Wiki.

## What it does

A structure on each Ethereal Islet (one per islet, 4x4, not movable). It
reverts a monster into attuned Meebs, then recombines Meebs into two new
monsters, which hatch in the attached Egg Cups. The process is destructive: the
input monster is lost.

- Each harmonizer has an infinite supply of its islet's Meeb.
- A fifth Element Meeb (the element not in the islet's Quad) can be conjured by
  combining one of each of the other Meebs. A small flash marks it.
- Single-Element monsters yield no Meebs. They only add to the infinite stash.
- Nicknames are split and scrambled between the two outputs.
- Speed-up: Diamonds or video ads (Help Center).

## Time

Higher level means more Meebs and longer time. Doubles run from 20h to about
1d 10h. Quads run from about 1d 2h to 1d 18h. The wiki table lists exact
values per level band.

## Dish Wishes

- Unlock: at least one monster of each element count (1 Single, 1 Double,
  1 Triple, 1 Quad) on the islet. The Primordial counts as a Single.
- Guarantees a chosen Double and/or Triple after enough attempts. Cannot target
  the islet's Single, Quad or Primordial.
- Extra Shard cost per attempt, listed above.
- Meter: each Double received fills 16.6% (six others needed), each Triple
  11.1% (nine others). Resets to 0 when the wished monster arrives.
- Changing the wish resets progress toward that element count.

## Primordials

- Requires every other Ethereal on the islet collected and at least 3 of each of
  the 5 Elements in the harmonizer.
- A harmonized Quad may upgrade to the Primordial. Only one Primordial per
  islet.
- Since Version 5.4.2 a meter on the Dish Wish page counts Quads produced. When
  full, the next Quad is guaranteed to become the Primordial.
- Raise Quad odds by harmonizing Quads and Triples before Doubles and Singles.

## Rarefied Dish-Harmonizer (Rarethereals)

- Upgrade cost: 100,000 Shards (wiki). The user reported a 50,000 Shard sale on
  2026-10-01.
- Unlocks a chance at Rarethereals on each harmonization.
- Rarethereals are only obtainable during special promotions, and the rare must
  be in the Market (Help Center). They have a much lower chance than commons.
- Harmonizing a Rare you already own significantly raises the chance of more.
- Dish Wishes do not cover Quads, so a rare Quad (such as Rare Whaill) is pure
  chance.
- Rare Whaill (Quad, Plasma) was added in version 5.2.0 on 2026-01-28 and is the
  first Quad Rarethereal. Epic Ethereals on the islets were teased but are not
  available.

## Strategy notes

- Upgrading only pays off if you will harmonize during a rare promotion window.
- Verify in-game whether the Rarefied upgrade is bought per islet.
- Higher-level and happier monsters earn more per minute.
